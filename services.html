Bookkeeping, QuickBooks & Payroll Services

Bookkeeping, QuickBooks, and payroll — handled by one accountant, start to finish.

Monthly Bookkeeping

The core of what we do. Each month we categorize every transaction, reconcile your bank and credit card accounts, and deliver a profit and loss report and balance sheet you can actually read. No more guessing whether you made money last month.

QuickBooks Online Setup & Cleanup

QuickBooks is only as good as the way it’s set up. We build company files with a chart of accounts that fits your business, connect your bank feeds, and train you on what to do — and what not to do — day to day.

Already have QuickBooks and it’s a mess? Cleanup is one of our most-requested services: years of miscoded transactions, duplicate entries, unreconciled accounts, balances that never quite made sense. We’ll scope it and tell you what it will take before you commit to anything.

Payroll

We run payroll on your schedule and prepare year-end W-2s and 1099s, so filings stay on schedule and your records stay clean.

Cash Flow & Financial Consulting

Cash flow projections, budgeting, or a second opinion before a big decision. Book time and get a straight answer instead of a sales pitch. Where a question calls for legal or investment advice, we’ll say so and point you to the right professional.

Pricing. Every business is different, so we quote each engagement after a free 20-minute consultation. Scope and fees are confirmed in writing before work begins.

Scope of services. RA Ledger Solutions is not a CPA firm and does not provide audit, attest, review, or compilation services. Monthly reports are prepared for internal management use from records and source documents supplied by the client. See our Privacy & Terms for full details.

How your information is handled. Documents and account access are requested only after a written engagement letter is in place, never through an unsecured form or email attachment. All data in transit is encrypted, and we do not take custody or control of client funds — your bank and payroll accounts stay in your name, under your control, and you authorize every transaction.
