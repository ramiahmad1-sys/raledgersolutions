Bookkeeping Services in Staten Island, NY

Staten Island small businesses need books that are current, accurate and easy to read. RA Ledger Solutions provides monthly bookkeeping for owners who want their numbers organized without hiring a full-time bookkeeper.

What monthly bookkeeping includes

  • Transactions categorized in QuickBooks Online each month
  • Bank, credit card and merchant account reconciliation
  • Profit and loss and balance sheet reports for internal management use

Who we work with

We work with Staten Island owners in service businesses, retail, professional practices, nonprofits and property management. Most of the work is done remotely through QuickBooks Online, so you do not need to visit an office. Documents and account access are requested only after a written engagement letter is in place.

Serving businesses across Staten Island

We work with small businesses throughout the borough, including St. George, Stapleton, Port Richmond, West Brighton, New Springville, New Dorp, Great Kills, Eltingville, Annadale, Tottenville and Charleston, as well as owners in Brooklyn and the rest of New York City. Because the work is done in QuickBooks Online, location is never a barrier.

Common questions from Staten Island owners

Do I need to bring paperwork to an office?

No. Most bookkeeping is handled remotely through QuickBooks Online and secure bank feeds, so there is no need to drop off boxes of receipts.

My books are months behind. Can you catch them up?

Yes. Catch-up and cleanup work is common. We review where your records stand, quote the cleanup in writing, and then move you onto a monthly schedule. See QuickBooks cleanup for details.

Book a free 20-minute consultation by calling 718-285-6959 or using the contact page. We will look at your current setup, tell you what it would take to get your books in order, and quote the work in writing before anything begins.

Important to know

RA Ledger Solutions is not a CPA firm and does not provide audit, attest, review or compilation services. We do not take custody or control of client funds; your bank accounts stay in your name.
