Privacy, Terms & Accessibility

Privacy

This website does not sell or share your personal information. Information you submit through the contact form (name, business name, email, phone, and message) is sent directly to RA Ledger Solutions via FormSubmit, a third-party form-delivery service, and is used only to respond to your inquiry.

RA Ledger Solutions does not take custody or control of client funds. Bank and payroll accounts remain in the client’s name and under the client’s control, and the client authorizes all disbursements.

Client financial documents are never requested or exchanged through this website. Document exchange for active engagements is arranged directly and separately once a working relationship begins.
