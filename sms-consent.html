SMS Alerts: Consent, Terms & Opt-Out

RA Ledger Solutions (a DBA of Alquds Accounting & Tax Services LLC), Staten Island, NY.

What these messages are

Our automated phone assistant answers inbound calls to the business. When a caller leaves a callback request, one internal text alert is sent to the business owner's own mobile number with the caller's name, callback number and reason for calling. Customers, callers and members of the public never receive text messages from this program. There is no marketing content.

How the business owner opts in

The owner enters their own mobile number as the alert destination in the phone assistant's settings. The settings screen displays the consent statement below, and alerts begin only after the owner agrees. The owner may also opt in by texting START or YES to the campaign number.

Alert destination number: ______________________

After opt-in, a confirmation text is sent: "RA Ledger Solutions: You are now subscribed to new-call text alerts from your RA Ledger phone assistant. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to cancel."

Sample message

RA Ledger Solutions: New call message. Caller: [name], callback [phone number]. Reason: needs bookkeeping help.

Opt-out and help

Reply STOP (also CANCEL, QUIT, END, UNSUBSCRIBE, STOPALL, OPTOUT, REVOKE) at any time to stop alerts, or remove the number in the assistant's settings. Reply START to resubscribe. Reply HELP or INFO for help, or contact info@raledgersolutions.com or 718-285-6959.

Terms

Message frequency varies with the number of callback requests received. Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of any purchase or service.

Privacy

Mobile numbers and opt-in information are used only to deliver these alerts. We do not sell, rent or share mobile numbers or SMS opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See our Privacy Policy.
